# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, constructive, and focused on learning and building.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and concise in technical discussions.
- Give feedback on code, design, and security risks rather than on people.
- Avoid sharing private keys, personal data, or confidential financial information.
- Follow the rules of the hackathon and the Casper developer community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private keys, wallet exports, secrets, or non-public personal data.
- Encouraging misuse of the project against real users, funds, or institutions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the project or community.
